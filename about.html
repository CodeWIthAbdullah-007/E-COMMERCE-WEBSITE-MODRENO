Who We Are

Furniture That Tells Your Story

MODERNO was founded with a simple belief: your home should reflect who you are. Every piece of furniture we create is a conversation between form and function.

From our workshop in Milan, our team of artisans and designers work together to craft pieces that aren't just functional — they're transformative.

What started as a small studio has grown into a brand trusted by over 12,000 customers worldwide.
